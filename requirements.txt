# Copernicus DEM tiles are Cloud Optimized GeoTIFFs. rasterio (BSD-3, so
# commercially clean like the rest of the stack) reads them over HTTP with range
# requests, so a 1 km site pulls tens of kilobytes rather than the whole 22 MB
# tile. Hand-parsing a tiled, compressed, float32 GeoTIFF is the kind of thing
# that produces plausible wrong elevations rather than errors.
rasterio>=1.4
